### 🔬 Research Interests

My primary research interests lie at the intersection of **AI for Software Engineering (AI4SE)** and **SE for AI (SE4AI)**. I am passionate about bridging the gap between complex AI systems and human developers.

* **Human-in-the-loop Verification:** Developing effective and user-friendly processes for humans to verify and interact with complex AI systems.
* **Next-Gen Coding Agents:** My current focus is on enhancing Coding Agents beyond simple chat logs. I aim to research methods that represent the **latent relationships between repository artifacts** and visualize the **potential impact of code changes**, enabling more robust and context-aware software evolution.
